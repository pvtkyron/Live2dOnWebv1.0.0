GitHub keeps the durable truth

The storefront, product pages, journal, styles, routing assets, SEO files and Live2D resources live in the repository. That makes the public surface portable and inspectable instead of locking the content into one publishing service.

Blogfa is a surface, not a dependency

The Blogfa integration can mount the GitHub-fed storefront while native Blogfa routes continue to exist for posts and archives. If that shell changes, the underlying Project Rev pages still have their own URLs.

Telegram handles what changes today

Availability, current commercial details, support coordination and live announcements belong in one human contact lane: @project_rev.

The design rule

Clarity before hype. Real routes before fake navigation. Useful context before a sales pitch. Personality is welcome; mystery is not.
