ABOUT THE MARKET

A loud storefront with a boringly sensible backbone.

Project Rev Market is a static-first catalog and publishing surface designed to be portable, inspectable, and easy for crawlers to understand.

What lives here

The shop, product pages, journal posts, Live2D mascot integration, styles, scripts, SEO metadata, sitemap and robots rules all live in GitHub.

What Blogfa does

Almost nothing. A compatibility template can point visitors and crawlers toward the canonical storefront, but no product or post needs Blogfa to exist.

What Telegram does

It is the official human contact lane for current availability, purchase questions, support coordination and announcements: @project_rev.
